How it works

A fitness tracker doesn’t walk for you. It shows you how you move.

Neuroditto doesn’t think for you. It shows you how you think.

01

A few words about something that happened today. No long journaling.

What it notices

Where you jump. Where you hesitate. Where you’re sure and wrong.

Jump

You react before you’ve heard the whole thing.

“You answered fast in three of your five work moments.”

Hesitate

You wait, even when you already know what to do.

“You rewrote that message four times before you sent it.”

Privacy

Your data stays on your phone.

Neuroditto is a phone app for one person: you. The moments you share stay on your device.

Why we’re building it

Most AI tools think for you. Neuroditto helps you think for yourself.

In a study of nearly 1,000 students, AI that gave answers made students score 17% worse once it was taken away. The same AI, built to give hints, did not. AI isn’t the problem. How it’s built is.

Bastani et al., PNAS, 2025

Questions people ask us

An AI to fix AI dependence?+

AI isn’t the problem; how it’s built is. Most AI tools think for you. Neuroditto shows you how you think, and you decide what to change.

Is it a therapist or a coach?+

No. It’s a tracker. It notices and shows. It doesn’t diagnose, and it doesn’t tell you what to do.

How much do I have to write?+

A few words per moment. The pattern comes from many small moments over weeks.

Where does my data go?+

It stays on your phone.
