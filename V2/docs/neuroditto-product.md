# What Neuroditto is (official product definition)

Source: Jyothi, Nov 3, 2026. This is the source of truth for what the product is and does. It wins over any older description in other Project docs or skills (for example "a private personal AI companion" in `reel-editor-SKILL.md` or older weekly packs).

## In Jyothi's words

**A fitness tracker for your mind: it tracks your thinking patterns and blind spots across every part of your life.**

You share a moment from your day in a few words. The app shows you how you reacted, and over weeks it shows the pattern: where you jump, where you hesitate, where you are sure and wrong. A phone app, for one person, with the data staying on their phone. The tracker sees. The companion speaks.

## The facts, one per line

- What it is: a fitness tracker for your mind.
- What it tracks: thinking patterns and blind spots, across every part of life (not only AI use).
- What the user does: shares a moment from the day in a few words.
- What the app shows right away: how the user reacted.
- What the app shows over weeks: the pattern. Where you jump, where you hesitate, where you are sure and wrong.
- Platform: a phone app.
- Who it is for: one person (personal, not a team or family tool).
- Privacy: the data stays on the user's phone.
- Roles: the tracker sees; the companion speaks.

## How to use this in content

- The product is the tracker. Never call the product itself a friend, companion, mentor, coach or therapist.
- The companion characters (Mira, Mochi, Sprout and the rest, see `companions-roster.md`) are the voice inside the app: they tell the user what the tracker saw. Showing a companion "speaking" a pattern on a card is correct.
- The three pattern words are strong hook and carousel material: **jump, hesitate, sure and wrong.** Use them as written.
- "A few words" and "over weeks" are honest limits: do not promise instant insight or long journaling.
- "Data stays on the phone" is a trust point for investors and careful users. Do not add claims beyond it (no "encrypted", "never online", "100% private") unless Jyothi or Krishna confirm.
- Stage: still ask for the latest stage before any update content. Never invent users or milestones.
