C

Calvin Cycle

— biology · cycles · biochemistry —

The light-independent waltz where carbon enters as a gas and leaves as a sugar — fixation, reduction, regeneration, repeat — using the ATP and NADPH that the light reactions stocked the kitchen with. RuBisCO welds CO₂ onto a five-carbon sugar; the unstable product splits into two molecules of 3-PGA, which are reduced to G3P. For every six turns, one G3P leaves to become glucose; the rest are recycled.
