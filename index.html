§ iv.

A workspace that thinks
with you.

Six ways Bloom turns scattered questions into compound knowledge. Every feature is opt‑in, every output is yours to export.

i.

Corrective RAG

An LLM grades every retrieval — correct, ambiguous, or off — and adapts. You get answers grounded in your sources, not hallucinations dressed in confidence.

ii.

A self‑growing knowledge graph

Concepts auto‑extract from every answer. Neo4j stores them; ForceAtlas2 lays them out. The more you ask, the richer the map of your own mind.

iii.

The Wiki that writes itself

Each concept node has a synthesized summary that compounds with every new question or source. You always have a clean, current page on what you've learned.

iv.

The Mirror

Patterns in your curiosity surfaced gently — depth vs breadth, dormant concepts, where you're stuck. A reflection, never a report card.

v.

Spaced review

Concepts you've barely touched bubble up. Concepts you've cracked rest. A quiet flashcard system tuned by your forgetting curve, not someone else's.

vi.

Export to git

Your wiki is just markdown. One click sends every concept page to your repo or Obsidian vault. We're a co‑pilot, not a roach‑motel.

§ ix.

Honest answers
before you ask.

If we missed yours, ask Bloom — it's good at this.

Does Bloom store my data on its own servers?+

By default, the cloud version stores your wiki encrypted at rest in the EU or US (your choice on signup). If you'd rather keep everything local, the entire stack runs on your machine via Docker — Neo4j, Qdrant, Ollama, the works.

Which AI models does it use?+

Anything you bring. The default cloud config uses GPT‑4o for synthesis and a local Llama 3.1 (via Ollama) for personality and concept extraction. You can swap in Claude, Gemini, Mistral — your keys, your choice.

Is the personality dashboard a gimmick?+

It's earnest but humble — based on the TCI 240 framework, with explicit confidence bands and a "this isn't me" button per dimension. It's a mirror for noticing patterns, not a label generator.

Can I export my wiki and walk away?+

Always. One click ships every concept page as markdown to your git repo, Obsidian vault, or a downloadable zip. Wikilinks, frontmatter, and the works. We'd rather earn the next month than trap you in last month's.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Notion AI?+

ChatGPT forgets between sessions. Notion AI remembers documents but not concepts. Bloom is a knowledge graph first — every answer becomes a node, every node grows a wiki page, every wiki page deepens future answers. It compounds.

Is there a student discount?+

Yes — verified students and educators get the Bloom plan free. Email us with proof and we'll flip the switch within a day.
